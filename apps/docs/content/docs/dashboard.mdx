Sign in with `ADMIN_EMAIL` and `ADMIN_PASSWORD` from your `.env`. The sidebar has these pages.

| Page | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | where you are |
| Queue | jobs you want to apply to |
| Stats | what you applied to and what came back |
| Notes | a notes board |
| Answers | your answer bank |
| Extension | create and revoke extension tokens |
| Gmail | import job alert emails, see [Gmail job emails](/docs/gmail) |
| Sources | watch careers pages and feeds, see [Job sources](/docs/sources) |
| Settings | your profile and account |

## Answers

The answer bank holds what skiffr fills for a question. Add, edit and delete answers, and see how often each was used and corrected. The suggestions panel proposes which learned answers to keep or drop, see [how skiffr learns](/docs/learning). Nothing is changed without you resolving it.

## Queue

Paste a job link, with an optional title and company, or let [Gmail](/docs/gmail) and [Sources](/docs/sources) fill it. Filter by status (queued, applied, skipped) and source, search by company or role, and use **Open next** to go through the queue in order. Mark a job applied or skipped, or restore it to the queue. The same posting is queued once, whatever way it arrived. You always press submit yourself.

## Stats

Applications per day and week, the queue funnel, replies by the furthest stage reached, breakdowns by source, ATS, match score and role, time to apply, and an autofill section: fields skiffr filled and whether you left, corrected or cleared them. Pick a range at the top. Percentages only show from 5 applications or filled fields, before that they would be noise. The autofill section comes from the learning events, which the extension does not record yet, so it stays empty for now.
