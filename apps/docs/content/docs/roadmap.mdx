This page is the product research behind the board ([#102](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/102)). It is opinionated on purpose and meant to be argued with. Every item on the roadmap is an issue on the [project board](https://github.com/users/FindMalek/projects/25).

## Vision

Applying for jobs is mostly retyping the same facts into forms that all behave differently. Tools that promise to do it for you tend to make things up, spam employers, hide behind a paywall and send your CV somewhere you cannot see. skiffr takes the other side:

1. **You stay the applicant.** It fills, you review, you press submit. It never invents a fact and never bypasses a captcha or a login.
2. **It gets better with use.** Corrections become answers, and it shows where every value came from.
3. **Your data stays yours.** Open source, self hosted, no analytics. A model is optional: it runs on your server with the key you configure, and it only sees what a feature needs, when you press it. Importing a CV sends the CV text, and you review the result before anything is saved.
4. **It covers the whole journey**, not only the form: finding jobs, checking they are real, the CV, the form, tracking replies, following up.

## Who it is for

| Persona | What they need | What would make them leave |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The active seeker (engineer, designer, analyst) applying to 5 to 20 jobs a week | Fast, correct fills on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday; tailored CV; a queue; knowing what got a reply | A wrong visa or salary answer, a form left half filled with no explanation |
| The international applicant or career changer | Work authorization per country, other languages, other date and phone formats, relocation answers | An English only tool that guesses |
| The privacy minded self hoster | Own Postgres, no telemetry, clear list of what the model sees, easy upgrades and backups | Data leaving without a switch, a broken upgrade |
| The coach or helper (later) | A read only view of a client's queue and stats | Needing the client's password or profile |
| The contributor | A clear way to add an ats adapter or a language | No contract, no starter issues, slow reviews |

## What job seekers say

Method and honest limits: Reddit blocks the research crawler, so there are no first hand Reddit threads here. The evidence is Hacker News threads, Trustpilot pages, Firefox add-on reviews, the README of an open source Workday filler, community forums of two password managers and a set of surveys. Trustpilot and add-on reviews are self selected and not fact checked: a claim about a named product below is what its reviewers report, with the month of the review, not a finding of ours. Many comparison blogs are written by competitors, and those are marked as such and used only where nothing better exists. Ratings and prices move: prices were read in November 2026, check the vendor before you trust a number.

## Edge cases and designs

Short answers to the hard cases, with where each lands.

- **Account walls (Workday and others).** skiffr never types a password and never creates an account. It detects the wall, says "your turn", and offers the fill again afterwards. A ledger of which tenant you have an account at, with the email used and no password, helps people who sign up for twenty companies ([#129](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/129), [#130](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/130)). An optional application email or alias in the profile keeps signups apart from the main inbox.
- **Resume upload.** The person picks the PDF once in the extension options, and confirms the attach on each form. The tailored PDF from CV tailoring can be pulled in per job. Cover letter and photo slots never get the resume ([#123](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/123)).
- **Cover letters and free text.** Drafts come from the profile, the tailored CV and the posting. Numbers, employers and dates must exist in the sources or the draft is refused. Drafts are outlined, labelled and undoable, and an edit is learned ([#124](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/124), [#125](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/125)).
- **Salary.** Never filled by default. A policy per person (range, target, never) and per currency. A "current salary" question stays empty and outlined, because asking is illegal in some places. A negotiation note per job stays local ([#126](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/126)).
- **Work authorization, relocation, remote.** Answered by rules from structured data, never by the model. Unknown means empty and outlined, never a default yes ([#118](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/118)).
- **Formats.** Dates follow the field first, the locale second, and stay empty when ambiguous. Phones are stored as E.164 and shaped per field. Names carry parts, a preferred name and a transliteration slot ([#119](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/119), [#107](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/107), [#121](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/121)).
- **Captcha and bot checks.** Never bypassed. Hand over to the person, then re arm ([#129](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/129)).
- **Duplicates and volume.** A warning, not a block: same company and title, a cooldown after a recent application, and a pacing prompt in "open next" ([#133](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/133), [#134](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/134)). skiffr will not become an auto apply bot: that is the line between it and the tools users complain about.
- **Ghost jobs.** Signals with reasons, never a hidden score, and one click to say "it is open" ([#132](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/132)).
- **Tracking.** Reply emails become suggestions the person confirms. skiffr stores the status, company and date, never the mail ([#135](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/135)). Reminders and deadlines come out as a private ics feed, with no calendar login ([#136](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/136)).
- **Learning quality.** Facts expire (notice period, availability), answers have a scope (this site, this ats, global) with a conflict view, and the person can mark a field "never learn" ([#116](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/116), [#117](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/117), [#115](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/115)).
- **Trust.** A panel with the source of each value, undo per field and for the whole fill, and a dry run ([#113](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/113), [#114](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/114)).
- **Failure modes.** Offline cache and queued learning, model down falls back to saved answers, a version handshake between extension and api, and a "this site did not work" report that shows exactly what it would share ([#139](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/139), [#112](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/112), [#140](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/140)).
- **Mobile and other browsers.** Extensions do not run on phones, so the phone adds jobs to the queue through a share target and the form is done on a laptop ([#142](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/142)). Edge and Firefox are builds, Safari is a plan ([#141](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/141)).
- **Data rights.** Export everything, delete everything, import JSON Resume and the LinkedIn archive ([#111](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/111), [#109](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/109)).
- **LinkedIn Easy Apply and Indeed Apply.** Open question, listed in the ideas below and not an issue yet. A person driven fill of those modals is not the same as a bot, but both sites forbid automation in their terms and users report restrictions. Until there is a clear reading of the terms skiffr does not touch them. Decide before building.

## Roadmap

Ordering rule: first value, then trust, then the pains people name most, then breadth. Nothing here makes skiffr submit for you.

### Ideas without an issue yet

- **Team and coach sharing.** A read only link to a person's queue and stats, with no profile and no answers. Large, depends on the data export work.
- **Interview prep sheet per application.** Questions and stories drawn from the person's own answer bank and the posting, never invented experience.
- **Learning quality replay.** A harness that replays correction logs and reports hit rate by source, correction rate and stale rate, run in ci on fixtures. Follows the recorded real model eval ([#41](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/issues/41)).
- **LinkedIn Easy Apply and Indeed Apply.** See the edge case above: a terms reading comes first, then maybe an issue.
- **Safari.** An app store build costs money and a native wrapper. Only after Edge and Firefox.
- **A browser independent fill bookmarklet** for pages where an extension is blocked. Low priority and hard to keep safe.

## How this page is kept

When an item ships, move it to the gap table as solved and delete the row from the roadmap. When a pain shows up in a bug report or a review more than twice, add it to the top 25 with the link. The reviewer guide in [`.github/REVIEW.md`](https://github.com/FindMalek/skiffr/blob/main/.github/REVIEW.md) is updated the same way with new bug classes.
